# Privacy policy: Repos Daily Poster

Repos Daily Poster is a private tool used by the owner of the Repos Daily channels to upload their own short videos to their own YouTube channel.

- It asks for access to one YouTube channel, the owner's, and uses it only to upload videos and set their title, description and privacy.
- It does not read, collect, store or share data about other people.
- The access token is kept privately by the owner and is never shared.
- Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: open an issue in this repository.
